## 1. Architecture as it stands

Two research layers exist today:

**Layer A — Bayesian regime engine (ETF universe, time-series allocation)**

```
prices (5 ETFs, 2005+)            sp500_top100_adj_close.csv (98 names, 2016+)
        │                                        │
src/regime_models.py                      [NEW] src/signal_lab.py
  BOCPD (cp, ERL)  HMM3 (forward-filtered)        cross-sectional signals + IC
        │                                        │
src/amr.py                                [NEW] src/robustness.py
  λ calibration (EWMA Omega, ERL-adaptive)        deflated Sharpe, offset dispersion
  AMR / CVaR-AMR / HRP / inv-vol optimisers
  vol-targeting overlay (plain + regime-adaptive)
        │
src/backtest.py + src/metrics.py + src/visualization.py
  weekly engine, 5 bps TC, Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar/α/β/IR
```

`src/bayesian.py` is the original PosteriorAlpha line: Ledoit-Wolf prior +
EWMA recent moments, Mahalanobis-divergence λ, posterior blend, max-Sharpe /
min-variance. The AMR line deliberately abandoned μ estimation; the Bayesian
line still estimates μ — keep both, they are different philosophies.

**Layer B — cross-sectional stock layer (new, started 2026-06)**
`run_signal_lab.py` evaluates signals on the S&P-100 panel by IC before any
backtest is allowed to exist.

## 2. What the knowledge base says about our existing work

| KB source | Verdict on our work |
|---|---|
| Man AHL (vol targeting) | Already implemented, including a novel regime-adaptive leverage cap. Keep. |
| Grinold & Kahn (breadth) | Our structural weakness: 5 ETFs rebalanced weekly ≈ very low breadth. With IC≈0.05, IR is capped near 0.1–0.2. The S&P-100 panel raises breadth ~20×; stock-level signals are where the fundamental law says our effort should go. |

| HXZ / survivorship | The S&P-100 CSV is the *current* index membership → survivorship-biased. Fine for signal-shape research (IC decay, interactions); not fine for absolute Sharpe claims. Flag in every report. |

## 3. Novel research ideas (ranked)

### Idea 1 — Regime-gated momentum (`regime_mom`) — STARTED
Stock-level BOCPD ERL gates the 12-1 momentum signal: a stock whose return
distribution just broke (short ERL) has a momentum score generated by a dead
regime, so we shrink it. This is Daniel-Moskowitz momentum-crash logic applied
cross-sectionally per stock instead of with an index-level state variable —
to our knowledge not in the literature, and it reuses our one genuinely
differentiated asset (the BOCPD machinery).
*Status:* implemented in `src/signal_lab.py`, first IC results in
`results/signal_lab_ic.csv`.
*Next:* if IC survives, test the complement (does ERL gate reversal too?),
then long-short quintile backtest with costs, then DSR.

### Idea 2 — ERL as a cross-sectional factor in its own right
Does regime age predict returns? Hypotheses cut both ways: stable-regime
stocks ≈ quality/low-vol-like (carry premium), fresh-break stocks ≈ lottery /
uncertainty (underperform). Evaluated as `erl_stability` in the signal lab.
If it works, it is a new defensive-style signal constructed purely from
prices.

### Idea 3 — Factor momentum on our own signal library (Ehsani-Linnainmaa)
Once ≥4 stock-level signals exist, time them by their own trailing 12-month
long-short performance. Uses the Open Source Asset Pricing insight that
factors themselves trend. Cheap to test on top of the signal lab output.

### Idea 4 — BOCPD-conditional factor exposure (bridge Layer A → Layer B)
Use index-level ERL/Omega (the v4 machinery) to switch the cross-sectional
book: momentum book in stable regimes, low-vol/reversal book after change
points. This is factor timing with a Bayesian online state variable instead
of the usual macro proxies.

### Idea 5 — HRP under regime signals
Swap `amr_weights` for `hrp_weights` inside the v4 wrapper (same λ, same
adaptive lookback). Tests whether the v-series gains come from the regime
signals or from the optimiser. Also serves as an ablation the KB demands.

### Idea 6 — Meta-labeling the rebalance decision (López de Prado ch. 3)
Train a small classifier on (cp, ERL, Omega, realized vol, turnover) to
predict whether next week's rebalance trade adds value vs. holding; size or
skip trades accordingly. Uses purged K-fold with embargo — the CV scheme is
non-negotiable.

## 4. Standing hygiene rules adopted (from KB §5)

1. IC analysis before any backtest (signal lab is the gate).
2. Every variant counts as a trial → `dsr_report` over the family before
   claiming any version improvement is real.
3. Every weekly strategy must report `rebalance_offset_dispersion`.
4. Survivorship caveat on all S&P-100 results until a point-in-time
   universe is sourced.
5. Costs: 5 bps × turnover minimum, as in the AMR engine, everywhere.

## 5. First signal-lab results (2026-06-12, 94 names, 2016-04 → 2026-04)

Monthly Spearman IC vs. forward returns; t-stats overlap-corrected for
horizons > 1 month. Full table in `results/signal_lab_ic.csv` (gitignored;
regenerate with `python run_signal_lab.py`).

| signal | IC 21d (t) | IC 63d (t) | IC 126d (t) | hit 21d | Q5−Q1 ann. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| momentum_12_1 | 0.024 (1.21) | 0.031 (0.95) | 0.037 (0.78) | 0.56 | +3.8% |
| reversal_1m | 0.004 (0.20) | 0.003 (0.10) | −0.005 (−0.12) | 0.52 | +2.3% |
| low_vol | −0.032 (−1.20) | −0.050 (−1.09) | −0.055 (−0.84) | 0.42 | −14.0% |
| erl_stability | −0.013 (−1.05) | −0.002 (−0.11) | 0.016 (0.53) | 0.45 | −2.5% |
| regime_mom | 0.021 (1.10) | 0.026 (0.87) | 0.027 (0.60) | 0.58 | +2.8% |

Honest read — **no signal passes the t > 2 bar on this sample**, and the lab
is doing its job by saying so before any backtest exists:

- The sample is underpowered by construction: ~107 monthly observations with
  IC vol ≈ 0.20 means even a true IC of 0.03 needs ~15 years for t = 2.
  Mega-cap-only universes are also where cross-sectional effects are weakest
  (lowest breadth, most efficient names).
- `momentum_12_1` has the right shape (positive IC growing with horizon,
  hit rate 0.56–0.59) at magnitudes consistent with the literature for
  large caps. Worth keeping as the base signal.
- `low_vol` is strongly *negative* in this decade — a current-membership
  S&P-100 over 2016–2026 is precisely the sample where high-vol winners
  (NVDA-style) were retained by the index filter. This is the survivorship +
  regime caveat made visible, not evidence against the anomaly.
- `regime_mom` (the novel gate) did not beat raw momentum on mean IC, but
  delivered a higher hit rate (0.58/0.60 vs. 0.56/0.59) with lower IC vol —
  the gate trades magnitude for consistency. Next test: gate only at
  *extreme* freshness (ERL < 21) instead of linearly, and test the gate on
  the short leg, where momentum crashes actually live.
- `erl_stability` alone is flat at short horizons with a sign flip at 126d —
  inconclusive, parked.

Trials ledger: 5 signals × 3 horizons on one sample. Carry this count into
any future DSR on strategies built from these signals.
