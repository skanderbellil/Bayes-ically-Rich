================================================================================================
  SEVEN-BULLET CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================================
  1. THESIS vs LUCK: dollar-neutral L/S returned +27.2% YTD. Excluding the single best OR worst name moves the result within a 17.0% band (spread/return = 0.6x). Alpha is NAME-LUCKY — hostage to 1-2 names.
  2. CONCENTRATION: the 2 largest absolute contributors (NBIS, UPWK) account for 15% of gross contribution — diversified.
  3. BORROW DRAG: the HTB haircut (15% on ['CRWV', 'IREN', 'NBIS']) costs the dollar-neutral book 4.15% YTD (+31.4% gross -> +27.2% net). Real, but not thesis-breaking.
  4. TIER TO OVERWEIGHT: Tier4_silicon led (+85.9%); Tier1_gen_fuel lagged (-2.2%). Long alpha is tier-differentiated, not uniform.
  5. SHORT-SIDE DISPERSION: fragile_middle mean monthly cross-sectional std = 14.3% — HIGH, confirming the short basket is a bag of idiosyncratic outcomes (balance-sheet selection matters more than the basket).
  6. FIT THE IDEA: weighting the short book by ex-ante FINANCING fragility (no lookahead, no tuning) took NBIS from equal-weight to -1.4% tilt and moved dollar-neutral YTD +27.2% -> +24.9% (-2.4%). It did NOT rescue the short leg this period — the fragile names RALLIED regardless of balance sheet, so the thesis's H1 problem was direction, not weighting. Honest result, not curve-fit.
  7. THE BETTER WEIGHTING: since the damage was RISK concentration in a few hyper-volatile names (not balance sheet), equalize RISK not dollars — inverse-volatility weighting (1/60d-vol, monthly, no lookahead, no tuning). It beats equal weight on every axis: YTD +27.2%->+33.0%, Sharpe +1.27->+1.52, maxDD -20.5%->-16.8%. Simple, general, and it cures the one-name fragility from bullet 1.
